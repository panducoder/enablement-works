## Sales training and forgetting

People forget training unless they practice and use it soon. The strongest evidence here is peer-reviewed learning research, not sales surveys, and it favors spaced practice and testing over re-reading.

| Statistic | Source (linked) | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Training professionals in 150 organizations estimated that 62% of employees apply training on the job immediately, 44% after six months and 34% after one year. | [Saks and Belcourt, Human Resource Management](https://doi.org/10.1002/hrm.20135) | 2006 |
| One week after reading a passage, people who had taken a recall test remembered 56% of it; people who restudied it remembered 42%. | [Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) | 2006 |
| With repeated testing, recall after one week was 61%, against 40% with repeated study, although the tested group read the passage 3.4 times and the study group 14.2 times. | [Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) | 2006 |
| Over one week, the study-only group forgot 52% of what it first recalled; the repeated-testing group forgot 14%. | [Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) | 2006 |
| A review of 839 assessments in 317 experiments found that the best gap between study sessions grows as the time until the test grows. | [Cepeda et al., Psychological Bulletin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/) | 2006 |
| The best gap between study sessions was about 20% to 40% of the test delay for a test one week away, and about 5% to 10% for a test one year away (more than 1,350 learners). | [Cepeda et al., Psychological Science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/) | 2008 |
| Organizational training has a medium-to-large effect on behavior on the job (mean effect size d = 0.62, meta-analysis). | [Arthur et al., Journal of Applied Psychology](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.88.2.234) | 2003 |
| 47% of sellers do not get enough roleplay practice before customer calls. (vendor survey) | [Salesforce State of Sales, 7th edition](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/) | 2026 |
| 51% of enablement teams trained all of their sellers in the previous 12 months (134 companies). (vendor survey) | [SBI and Revenue Enablement Society benchmarks](https://index.sbigrowth.com/hubfs/Modern%20Enablement%20Benchmarks%20Report%20-%20February%202025.pdf) | 2025 |
| US employees received 40 hours of training a year in 2025, down from 47 hours the year before. | [Training magazine, Training Industry Report](https://trainingmag.com/2025-training-industry-report/) | 2025 |

The often-quoted claim that "reps forget 70% of training within a week" has no primary source we could find, so it is not on this page.

## How to use these numbers

Use external statistics to frame a decision, then replace them with your own data. A benchmark tells a CRO the problem is common. Only your numbers tell them it is yours.

1. **Pick one number per argument.** One clear statistic with its source beats five stacked ones. Name the publisher and year in the sentence, for example "Gartner's 2026 buyer survey found…".
2. **Check the base.** "67% of buyers" and "67% of sales organizations" are different claims. Read the sample size and who answered before you quote.
3. **Separate surveys from measurements.** Most rows here are self-reported. Time-saved and quota-attainment figures are what people said, not what a system recorded.
4. **Treat "X times more likely" as correlation.** Gartner's 3.7 times figure for AI and quota does not prove AI caused the result. Top sellers may simply adopt tools sooner.
5. **Label predictions.** A forecast for 2028 is not a finding. Say "Gartner predicts", not "Gartner found".
6. **Build your own baseline next.** Measure ramp, coaching coverage and completion before a program starts. The [four levels of evidence](/guides/sales-enablement-metrics/) show what to measure at each stage.

## Methodology

We checked every statistic on 6 November 2026. Each number was confirmed in the linked source, or in the publisher's own press release or announcement for that research, before it was added. We dropped any number we could not trace to a primary or publisher source, including several widely repeated sales statistics.

- **Year** is the year the source was published. Survey fieldwork is often the year before.
- **(vendor survey)** marks research published by a company that sells sales or enablement software or services. **(community survey)** marks research from a practitioner membership group. Unmarked rows come from analyst firms, an industry publication or peer-reviewed journals.
- Where a source gives a sample size, we include it. Percentages are as published; we have not recalculated them.
- Gartner and Forrester full reports are paywalled. For those rows we rely on the publisher's public press release.
- We will review this page each quarter and replace figures when newer editions are published.
