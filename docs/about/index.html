About Enablement Works

Enablement Works is the production and operations engine for B2B sales enablement teams. Your team sets the strategy. We build the content, roleplays and programs in your tools, run them, drive completion and report results to your CRO every month.

What we do

We work with B2B sales organizations of 100 to 1,000 reps whose enablement team is too small for the plan. We start with a three-week Enablement Diagnostic and continue with the Enablement Engine, a 12-month subscription for production and program operations. We work in the tools you already own and in your methodology. We do not resell licences.

What we believe

  • Strategy is the scarce part. Enablement leaders should spend their hours on judgment: which behaviors matter, which programs come first. Production and operations can be done by others, to their standard.
  • The asset is the cheap part. AI made content cheap. Launching it, driving completion, getting managers to reinforce it and showing the CRO what changed are the expensive parts.
  • Report evidence, not activity. Activity, practice, behavior and outcomes, on one page, every month.

Editorial standards for our guides

  • Every guide opens with a short, direct answer, then shows the method, a worked example and the limits.
  • Every external statistic links to its public source. Numbers we could not verify are left out. Our own rules of thumb are labelled as ours.
  • Worked examples use made-up, round numbers and say so. We never publish client names, client results or testimonials without written permission.
  • Guides show the date they were last updated. We review them at least every quarter.
  • Spotted an error? Email hello@enablement.works and we will correct it and note the change.
