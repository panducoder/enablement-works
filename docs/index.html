01The problem

The sales plan grew. The enablement team did not.

Every year the plan asks for more: onboarding for new hires, launch training, a kickoff, certifications, new talk tracks. A team of one to four people cannot build all of it and also run it.

So the work slips. Programs ship late. The kickoff gets no follow-up. Reps do not finish what was built. The platforms you pay for sit half-used. And the CRO sees activity counts, not results.

The problem is not the people. It is the hours.

02How it works

Diagnose, build, then run and report.

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    In three weeks we measure what your sales plan needs from enablement, what your team can deliver, what your tools return and which sales behaviors are missing. You get a 12-month roadmap with the first two programs defined.

  2. 2

    Build

    We produce what your roadmap needs, in your methodology and in the tools you already own: microlearning, AI roleplays, talk tracks, battlecards, launch kits, onboarding and certification programs. Your enablement lead approves every brief. A senior reviewer approves every asset before you see it.

  3. 3

    Run and report

    We launch each program with a message from your sales leader, nudge reps in Slack or Teams, send managers the list of reps who have not started, and escalate when completion is low. Each month your CRO gets one page: activity, practice, behavior and outcomes.

04Questions

Questions CROs and enablement leaders ask

Are you replacing our enablement team?

No. Your enablement team sets the strategy, chooses the programs, approves every brief and presents the results to the CRO. We are the production and operations team behind them.

Which tools do you work in?

Yours. We work in depth in Mindtickle, Highspot, Seismic, Gong and Second Nature, and in most LMS platforms. We do not resell licenses, and in our first year we take no referral fees from any vendor, so our advice stays neutral.

Do we need to record sales calls?

No. If you record calls, we can measure behaviors on real calls. If you do not, we measure with roleplay scores, certifications, manager observations and CRM data.

How do you decide how much work is included?

Your contract sets a monthly production volume from a fixed menu of assets, and a limit on how many briefs are in production at once. You see what is used and what is left at any time. Large items, such as a full onboarding program, can also be bought as a fixed-fee project.

How do you keep the content right for our company?

We keep your methodology, personas, approved claims and scorecard in one client kit. Every asset is checked against it, approved by a senior reviewer, and then reviewed by your named approver in two consolidated rounds. Nothing goes live without your approval.

How much of our time does it take?

In the first 30 days: about 3 to 4 hours of the CRO's time and 2 to 3 hours a week from the head of enablement. Managers and reps give 30 to 45 minutes each for interviews. After that, a 30-minute work review every one or two weeks and a 15-minute CRO report each month.

How do you handle our data and AI?

We use AI models through enterprise agreements with zero data retention, and your data is never used to train a model. We sign a data protection agreement and share our sub-processor list. Sales behavior scores are for coaching only and are never used in performance reviews. You own all content and data and can export it at any time.

What are the contract terms?

The Diagnostic is a fixed fee, 50% at signature. The Engine is a 12-month term billed each quarter in advance, with 90 days' notice to cancel after the first quarter. You can pause once a year for up to two months.
