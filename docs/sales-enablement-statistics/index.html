Sales enablement statistics for 2026: 50+ numbers with sources

MeasurementUpdated 6 November 202619 min readBy the Enablement Works editorial team

Short answer

The 2026 sales enablement numbers point one way. Most B2B buyers now prefer a rep-free experience (Gartner: 67%). Training fades without practice and reinforcement. AI saves sellers about 4.8 hours a week, but 72% of sales organizations do not reinvest that time (Gartner). Every statistic below links to its source.

Key takeaways

  • Gartner's 2026 buyer survey found 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience, up from 61% a year earlier.
  • Forrester counts 13 internal stakeholders and nine external influencers in a typical B2B buying decision.
  • Average account executive ramp reached 6.2 months in The Bridge Group's 2026 study, the longest in its history.
  • In an SBI and Revenue Enablement Society survey, the median enablement person supports 41 to 84 sellers, depending on team size.
  • Gartner found AI saves sellers 4.8 hours a week, yet 72% of sales organizations do not reinvest the time in high-value work.

This page collects 83 sales enablement statistics, each checked against a public source on 6 November 2026. Each row names the source, links to it and gives the year it was published. Research from companies that sell sales or enablement software or services is marked "(vendor survey)". Predictions are marked as predictions.

Buying groups and buyer behavior

Sales training and forgetting

People forget training unless they practice and use it soon. The strongest evidence here is peer-reviewed learning research, not sales surveys, and it favors spaced practice and testing over re-reading.

Statistic Source (linked) Year
Training professionals in 150 organizations estimated that 62% of employees apply training on the job immediately, 44% after six months and 34% after one year. >Saks and Belcourt, Human Resource Management 2006
One week after reading a passage, people who had taken a recall test remembered 56% of it; people who restudied it remembered 42%. >Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science 2006
With repeated testing, recall after one week was 61%, against 40% with repeated study, although the tested group read the passage 3.4 times and the study group 14.2 times. >Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science 2006
Over one week, the study-only group forgot 52% of what it first recalled; the repeated-testing group forgot 14%. >Roediger and Karpicke, Psychological Science 2006
A review of 839 assessments in 317 experiments found that the best gap between study sessions grows as the time until the test grows. >Cepeda et al., Psychological Bulletin 2006
The best gap between study sessions was about 20% to 40% of the test delay for a test one week away, and about 5% to 10% for a test one year away (more than 1,350 learners). >Cepeda et al., Psychological Science 2008
Organizational training has a medium-to-large effect on behavior on the job (mean effect size d = 0.62, meta-analysis). >Arthur et al., Journal of Applied Psychology 2003
47% of sellers do not get enough roleplay practice before customer calls. (vendor survey) >Salesforce State of Sales, 7th edition 2026
51% of enablement teams trained all of their sellers in the previous 12 months (134 companies). (vendor survey) >SBI and Revenue Enablement Society benchmarks 2025
US employees received 40 hours of training a year in 2025, down from 47 hours the year before. >Training magazine, Training Industry Report 2025

The often-quoted claim that "reps forget 70% of training within a week" has no primary source we could find, so it is not on this page.

How to use these numbers

Use external statistics to frame a decision, then replace them with your own data. A benchmark tells a CRO the problem is common. Only your numbers tell them it is yours.

  1. Pick one number per argument. One clear statistic with its source beats five stacked ones. Name the publisher and year in the sentence, for example "Gartner's 2026 buyer survey found…".
  2. Check the base. "67% of buyers" and "67% of sales organizations" are different claims. Read the sample size and who answered before you quote.
  3. Separate surveys from measurements. Most rows here are self-reported. Time-saved and quota-attainment figures are what people said, not what a system recorded.
  4. Treat "X times more likely" as correlation. Gartner's 3.7 times figure for AI and quota does not prove AI caused the result. Top sellers may simply adopt tools sooner.
  5. Label predictions. A forecast for 2028 is not a finding. Say "Gartner predicts", not "Gartner found".
  6. Build your own baseline next. Measure ramp, coaching coverage and completion before a program starts. The four levels of evidence show what to measure at each stage.

Methodology

We checked every statistic on 6 November 2026. Each number was confirmed in the linked source, or in the publisher's own press release or announcement for that research, before it was added. We dropped any number we could not trace to a primary or publisher source, including several widely repeated sales statistics.

  • Year is the year the source was published. Survey fieldwork is often the year before.
  • (vendor survey) marks research published by a company that sells sales or enablement software or services. (community survey) marks research from a practitioner membership group. Unmarked rows come from analyst firms, an industry publication or peer-reviewed journals.
  • Where a source gives a sample size, we include it. Percentages are as published; we have not recalculated them.
  • Gartner and Forrester full reports are paywalled. For those rows we rely on the publisher's public press release.
  • We will review this page each quarter and replace figures when newer editions are published.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free buying experience?

Gartner's survey of 646 B2B buyers, run in August and September 2025, found 67% prefer a rep-free experience. The year before, the figure was 61%. The same research found 69% of buyers still prefer to check AI-generated insights with a sales rep.

How many people are in a B2B buying group?

Forrester's State of Business Buying 2024 found an average of 13 people inside the buying organization, with 89% of purchases involving two or more departments. Its 2026 report adds nine external influencers to the 13 internal stakeholders. 6sense's 2024 vendor survey reported an average of 11.

How long does it take a new sales rep to ramp?

The Bridge Group's 2026 study puts average account executive ramp at 6.2 months, the longest in its history. Its 2025 study puts SDR ramp at 3.0 months. In an SBI survey of 134 companies, the median expected seller ramp was 3 months, and 24% expected 6 months or more.

How many sellers does one enablement person support?

An SBI and Revenue Enablement Society survey from 2025 found a median of 41 sellers per enablement FTE on teams of 2 to 4 people, 53 on teams of 5 to 9, and 84 on teams of 10 or more. Our own rule of thumb is one enablement person per 25 to 50 reps, depending on how much onboarding, launch and coaching work the plan needs.

How quickly do salespeople forget training?

There is no reliable sales-specific forgetting rate, so treat claims like '70% forgotten in a week' with care. Peer-reviewed research is clearer: in Roediger and Karpicke's 2006 study, people who restudied material recalled 42% a week later, against 56% for people who took a practice test. Training professionals in a 2006 study estimated only 34% of employees still apply training a year later.

How much time does AI save salespeople?

Gartner's 2026 survey of 210 sales leaders found AI tools save sellers an average of 4.8 hours a week. It also found 72% of sales organizations fail to reinvest that time in high-value activities. Salesforce's 2026 research found sellers expect AI agents to cut prospect research time by 34%.
