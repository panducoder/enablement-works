Follow-up email before you leave the call

The problem

Follow-ups go out late and generic, and buyers forget what they agreed to.

25 minutes per follow-up.

The value

A recap written in the buyer's own words, with next steps, owners and dates, sent while the call is still fresh.

2 minutes.

The move

From this {call transcript}, write a follow-up to {buyer} that restates their 2 priorities in their own words, confirms next steps with owners and dates, and stays under 140 words.
