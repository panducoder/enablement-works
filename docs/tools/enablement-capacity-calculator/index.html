How the calculator works

The calculator adds up the enablement hours your sales plan asks for, then divides by about 1,400 productive hours per enablement person per year. It compares the result with your team and with our rule of thumb of one enablement person for every 25 to 50 quota-carrying reps.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good sales enablement to rep ratio?

Our rule of thumb is one enablement person for every 25 to 50 quota-carrying reps. Move toward 25 when you hire many new reps, launch often or support coaching closely. Move toward 50 when the team is stable and programs are mature.

How many hours does one enablement person have in a year?

About 1,400 productive hours, after meetings, admin, holidays and leave. Planning on 2,000 hours is the most common reason enablement plans overrun.

What should I do if the calculator shows a gap?

Show both numbers to your CRO before the plan is final: the hours the plan needs and the hours the team has. Then either cut scope, add headcount, or move production and operations work to a partner so the team keeps the strategy.
