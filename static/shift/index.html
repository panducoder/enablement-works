Questions sellers ask first

What is The Shift?

The Shift is a library of short films that show AI workflows for B2B sales teams. Every film comes with the exact prompt, the tools it runs on, and the before and after it produced.

Who is it for?

Sales reps who want a faster way to prepare, front-line managers who coach, enablement teams who build programmes, and sales leaders who need proof before they scale a new habit.

Can I use the prompts?

Yes. Every video page has a Steal this prompt button. The highlighted parts are placeholders for your own account, persona or files.

Are the numbers real?

Not yet. This is a sample library: the workflows, clips and before and after figures are illustrative until real team videos are added.

How do shared links look?

Each video has its own share card with the before and after on it. Slack, Teams and LinkedIn show it when a link is pasted, and there is embed code for learning platforms.
